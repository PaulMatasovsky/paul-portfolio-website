ADR-001: Implementing a Project Journal

Date: 2026-08-14

Status: Accepted.

Context: I started building the portfolio website with the intent of changing careers, which means recruiters and hiring managers will be looking at my projects.
I am rebuilding this website from the ground up, and ADRs are only keeping part of the documentation.
ADRs track major decisions, but building or rebuilding a portfolio website also involves learning, changing perspectives, and realizing mistakes.
In other words, ADRs do not track personal growth, which, I believe, a potential recruiter or hiring manager would find interesting.

Decision: I believe that a project journal is the best supplementary tool for tracking my learning, changes in perspective, and personal growth.

Alternatives Considered: Not keeping any record.

Reasoning: ADRs only document the major architectural decisions I made.
I wanted to document my learning, realizations, and changes in perspective for this project, as it helps the reader get a better understanding of how I think.
I believe that recruiters and hiring managers would want to see how I grew and changed because of this project.
