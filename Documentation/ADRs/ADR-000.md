ADR-000: Architecture Decision Record Process

Date: 2026-08-09
Status: Accepted

Context:
I wanted to record the major design choices I made in this project because there are often several ways to achieve the same goal, and I wanted to show that I have looked for and carefully considered some of those options. I want others to see that there was intention and reasoning put into the decisions, that the alternatives were considered, and that the decision was based on the information, requirements, and circumstances I had known of at the time (e.g., time constraints). I am trying to be consistent in my documentation of the design choices because I am not naturally gifted at documentation, and would like to improve that skill.

Decision:
The following decisions were made regarding ADRs: ADRs are meant for meaningful decisions with consequences beyond the immediate task. These include things like architectural or organizational choices. ADRs will be as brief as possible while still fully documenting the decision. This is a skill in progress for me, so I will be endeavoring to write the ADR as soon as I make the decision. I will only document the alternatives I seriously researched and genuinely considered. ADRs will live with the project they are for, as opposed to keeping a single log of all ADRs for anything related to the website. All ADRs will start at 000, for their respective area. This ADR process will be used consistently throughout the entire project. I will use AI to help me research alternatives, explore tradeoffs, challenge assumptions and conduct the ADR interview, but I write the final ADR prose myself. Once an ADR is accepted, it will not be changed except for changing the status if it becomes superseded in the future. A new ADR records the new decision, and the old ADR’s status is changed to Superseded.

Alternatives Considered:
I considered not having a formal ADR process, creating much more comprehensive/formal ADRs, and potentially allowing AI to produce the finished documentation. 

Reasoning:
I decided on a formal ADR process that is only highlighting the essentials, not one that is overly comprehensive or bureaucratic, as this is closer to my own thought process. I decided to not let AI produce the finished documentation because it is easy for anyone to push a button and produce a result. I wanted my own voice to clearly shine through. 

The format for ADRs will be as follows:

ADR-nnn: Title of the Decision being Made

Date: yyyy-mm-dd

Status: (Accepted or Superseded)
As this is a solo project, I have slimmed the number of statuses down to only what I need for this project.

Context: 
The context surrounding the decision that was made. 

Decision:
The decision that was ultimately made. 

Alternatives Considered:
The alternatives that I seriously researched and genuinely considered will be listed here. 

Reasoning: 
Why the decision was made.

Consequences (optional): 
What consequences I am accepting by making this choice (e.g., choosing to use WordPress means a dependency on plugins).
