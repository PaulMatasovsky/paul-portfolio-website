## Brief user interview prompts

1. When the computer becomes confusing, what do you usually do?
2. What are the three most common tasks that you want help with?
3. Would you rather type your question, talk, or choose a large button?
4. When getting instructions, do you prefer one step at a time?
5. Would you be comfortable explicitly sharing the visible screen with a local helper?
6. What would help you trust that the helper is not changing things without asking?
7. When the helper does not understand, whom or what should it suggest?
