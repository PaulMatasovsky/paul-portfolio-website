## What I Covered

I followed the Lesson 2 plan stored in Google Docs and covered all of the core material. The lesson used Scratch to explore sequences and events, repeating instructions, a changing counter, prediction and debugging, and an introductory if/else decision. It also connected clear instructions and testing to AI assistance.

We spent most of the class playing with Scratch and talking through the lesson as questions and discoveries came up. The concepts came into the conversation naturally while the students experimented.

One student had a sprite moving around, but it looked as though it was floating. That brought up how changing a sprite’s costumes—different pictures or poses of the same character—can simulate walking or other movement. This connected the programming to something the student wanted the character to do.

I used the word cards again and referred back to the Adventure World demonstration. These gave us another way to revisit familiar ideas while working through the Scratch lesson.
